Raw Screen Limitations
Low SNR ≈ 8 dB
Screen brightness ~150 nits vs DLP projector ~2000 nits. Ambient light overwhelms fringe signal under typical room lighting.
Gamma Nonlinearity
LCD gamma (γ≈2.2) corrupts sinusoidal purity, introducing harmonic errors in wrapped phase computation.
Differential HDR Method
Ambient Subtraction
Capture Iambient before projection. Subtract per-pixel before phase computation: Iactive = Ipattern − Iambient.
Multi-Exposure Fusion
Capture at multiple exposures; fuse using HDR weights w(I) = exp(−(I−128)²/128²) to maximize dynamic range.
